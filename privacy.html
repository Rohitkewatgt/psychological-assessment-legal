Privacy Policy

Psychological Assessment Tool

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Psychological Assessment Tool handles information when you visit and use the website. The tool provides an educational self-assessment intended to support self-reflection. It is not a clinical or diagnostic instrument.

1. Information We Collect

The assessment is designed to calculate results from the answers you select while using the website. The assessment functionality does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal identifiers to complete the assessment.

The project does not intentionally collect or maintain a database of assessment responses through the assessment functionality. However, technical information may be processed by the website's hosting provider and third-party services as described below.

2. How Assessment Responses Are Processed

When you complete the assessment, your selected answers are processed within your browser to calculate category scores, percentages, and your result profile.

The assessment temporarily holds your responses in the browser's active JavaScript memory while the assessment is running. These responses are used for calculations and to generate your results.

The assessment code does not save responses to browser localStorage, sessionStorage, or a project-owned database. The assessment and scoring code does not send your questionnaire responses to a project-owned server.

When you reset or reload the assessment, or close the page, the in-memory assessment data is cleared as the page's JavaScript environment ends or resets. This describes the application's handling of its in-memory data and is not a guarantee about information that may independently exist in browser, operating system, or hosting-provider records.

Important: These statements describe the assessment's response-processing functionality. They do not mean that the website makes no network requests or that its hosting provider receives no technical information.

3. Result Images and Sharing

The website provides functionality to download or share an image of your assessment results. Generating this image is intended to take place in your browser.

If you choose to share the image, the image may be passed to the sharing destination you select. That destination may process the image and its contents under its own privacy policy.

You are responsible for deciding whether to share your results. Please review the contents of a result image before sharing it, particularly if it contains information you consider personal.

4. Website Hosting and Third-Party Services

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you access the website, GitHub and its infrastructure may process technical information associated with serving the website, such as IP addresses, request information, and other technical data, in accordance with their applicable policies.

The website also loads the html2canvas JavaScript library from the cdnjs content delivery network to support the result-image feature. Loading this external resource involves a request to a third-party service. That service may receive technical information associated with the request.

The assessment code reviewed for this project does not transmit questionnaire responses to these services as part of its scoring calculations. However, the handling of technical information by external providers is governed by their own policies.

You can review the relevant providers' policies here:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

5. Academic Research and Google Forms

If a separate Google Form is used for academic research, feedback, or data collection associated with this project, that form is separate from the website's assessment functionality.

Information submitted through a separate form is subject to the information provided with that form and the applicable policies of Google. Please review the form's purpose and privacy information before submitting any responses.

6. Data Security and Limitations

Processing assessment answers in browser memory reduces the need for the assessment to retain those answers on a project-owned server. However, no website or computing environment can guarantee absolute security or the complete absence of technical data processing.

Avoid entering sensitive personal information into assessment answers or sharing result images if you are uncomfortable with their contents being seen by others.

7. Children's Privacy

This assessment is intended for individuals aged 17–25. It is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. The project does not intentionally seek to collect children's personal information through the assessment functionality.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website's functionality, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page, along with its revision date.
