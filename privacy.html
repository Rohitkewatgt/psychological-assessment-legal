Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Psychological Assessment Tool, an educational project designed to help individuals aged 17–25 reflect on their behavioral tendencies.

This page is a draft until the assessment's data handling practices have been checked and this policy has been finalized.

1. Purpose of the Assessment

The assessment is intended for educational purposes and personal reflection. It is not a clinical diagnostic tool and should not be used as a substitute for professional psychological assessment or medical advice.

2. Information You Provide

While using the assessment, you may provide answers to questionnaire questions. The assessment may also process those answers to calculate category scores and display a result.

The information actually collected, retained, or transmitted depends on the website's implementation and any services used to operate it. The project owner should verify these details before this policy is finalized.

3. How Information Is Used

Questionnaire responses are intended to support the assessment's scoring process and presentation of results. Any additional use of responses, including academic research or analysis, should be clearly explained to participants before they take part.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

This policy does not make a blanket claim that information is never stored or shared. The project owner should identify whether responses or other information are stored in a database, collected through a separate form, retained by the browser, or processed by third-party services.

Any collection, retention, or sharing of personal information should be limited to the stated purpose and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party services for hosting or particular features. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies. The services actually used should be identified before this section is finalized.

6. Your Choices and Questions

Before participating, you should be able to understand what information is requested and how it will be used. If the project collects or retains identifiable information, the final policy should explain how participants can raise privacy questions or request appropriate action concerning their information.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the assessment's features or data handling practices change. The published version should reflect the practices currently in place.

8. Contact

For questions about this project or its privacy practices, use the contact method provided by the project owner. A contact method should be added here before publication.
