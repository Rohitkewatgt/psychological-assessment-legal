Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Psychological Assessment Tool, an educational project designed primarily for individuals aged 17–25.

By using the assessment, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this policy.

1. Information Processed by the Assessment

The assessment asks questions about your preferences, reactions, and typical behavior. Your selected answers are processed to calculate category scores and generate your result.

The assessment is designed to perform these calculations in your browser. Questionnaire answers are not intended to be submitted to a project-owned server or stored in a project-owned database.

2. Temporary Browser Storage

The assessment may use temporary browser session storage to maintain information needed while you complete the assessment. This information is stored in your browser rather than in a project-owned database.

Session storage generally remains available within the relevant browser tab until that tab's session ends or the stored information is cleared. Browser behavior may vary, so you should not treat closing a tab as a guaranteed secure deletion method.

3. Collection and Sharing

The assessment is not designed to transmit your questionnaire answers to the project owner or share those answers with other users.

This does not mean that using the website involves absolutely no data processing. The hosting provider, your browser, or other services involved in loading website resources may process technical information as part of providing their services.

The project owner does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or other direct identifiers through the assessment questionnaire unless a feature is added that specifically requests such information.

4. Hosting and Third-Party Services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and other infrastructure providers may process technical information associated with website requests in accordance with their own policies.

The assessment may also load certain resources from third-party services, such as a content delivery network used for a website feature. When a browser requests an external resource, that service may receive technical information such as the request and network information.

These services operate separately from the assessment itself. Their handling of technical information is governed by their own policies.

5. Academic Research and Separate Forms

This policy covers the assessment website. If a separate questionnaire or Google Form is used for academic research or data collection, that form is a separate activity and is not automatically covered by the website's browser-only processing.

Before participating in a separate data-collection activity, you should be informed about what information is requested, why it is collected, who can access it, how it will be used, and how long it will be retained.

6. Children's and Young People's Information

The assessment is designed primarily for individuals aged 17–25. Anyone who is under the age of 18 should use it only where permitted by applicable law and any relevant institutional requirements.

7. Data Security

The assessment is designed to minimize the transfer of questionnaire answers by processing them in the browser. You should nevertheless avoid entering identifying or sensitive personal information into questionnaire responses.

No website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the assessment's features, hosting arrangements, or information-handling practices change. The published policy should reflect the website's actual operation.
