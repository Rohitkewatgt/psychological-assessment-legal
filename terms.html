Terms of Use

These Terms of Use apply to the Psychological Assessment Tool, an educational project designed primarily for individuals aged 17–25.

1. Purpose of the Assessment

The assessment is intended to help users reflect on selected behavioral tendencies and preferences through a questionnaire and a scoring system developed for this educational project.

2. Not a Clinical Assessment

The assessment is not a clinical diagnostic instrument and is not presented as a scientifically validated psychological test. It does not diagnose mental health conditions or determine a person's overall personality, intelligence, abilities, or worth.

The results should not be used as a substitute for professional psychological assessment, medical advice, or treatment. If you have concerns about your mental health or need professional guidance, consult a qualified psychologist, doctor, or other appropriate professional.

3. Understanding Your Results

Results are calculated from your answers using the project's scoring rules. Category scores and written descriptions indicate tendencies suggested by those answers; they are not definitive conclusions about you.

The score ranges and categories are part of the project's design. They should not be interpreted as clinically established thresholds or proof of a psychological characteristic.

4. Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. Answer questions according to your usual preferences and behavior. You may stop using the assessment at any time.

Do not enter identifying, confidential, or sensitive personal information into answers unless the website explicitly requests it and explains why it is needed.

5. Privacy

Your use of the website is also subject to the Privacy Policy, which explains how the assessment is designed to process questionnaire responses and describes the role of hosting and third-party services.

Any separate academic research form or data-collection activity is distinct from the assessment website and may have its own information-handling arrangements.

6. Appropriate Use

You agree not to deliberately interfere with the website's operation, misuse its features, or present its results to others as a professional diagnosis or a validated psychological evaluation.

7. Changes and Availability

The questions, categories, scoring rules, descriptions, and features may change as the educational project develops. The website may occasionally be unavailable during maintenance or updates.

8. Limitations

The assessment is provided for educational and self-reflection purposes. Results may not fully represent an individual's behavior or circumstances. The project does not guarantee that a result will be complete, accurate, or suitable for every purpose.
