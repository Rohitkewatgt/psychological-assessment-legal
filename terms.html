Terms of Use

These Terms of Use explain the conditions for using the Psychological Assessment Tool, an educational project intended primarily for individuals aged 17–25.

The assessment is designed for educational purposes and personal reflection. It is not a clinically validated psychological test and does not provide a medical diagnosis.

1. Purpose and Scope

The assessment presents questionnaire-based results relating to selected behavioral tendencies. Its purpose is to encourage self-reflection and support an educational project. It is not intended to evaluate a person's overall worth, intelligence, mental health, or suitability for a particular activity.

2. No Medical or Psychological Diagnosis

The results are not a substitute for an assessment by a qualified psychologist, psychiatrist, doctor, or other appropriately qualified professional.

If you have concerns about your mental health or need professional guidance, consult a qualified professional. Do not make medical or mental-health decisions solely on the basis of this assessment.

3. Understanding Your Results

Results are generated from your questionnaire responses using the scoring rules designed for this project. The displayed categories and descriptions represent suggested tendencies, not definitive conclusions about your personality or behavior.

Different answers, circumstances, and interpretations may produce different results. The scoring thresholds used by this project should not be interpreted as scientifically established psychological standards.

4. Voluntary Participation

Participation should be voluntary. Answer questions honestly according to your usual preferences, and do not provide information you are uncomfortable sharing. You may stop using the assessment at any time.

5. Privacy

Your use of the assessment is also subject to its Privacy Policy. Review that policy to understand how information is handled. Do not assume that information is anonymous or never stored unless the published privacy information and actual implementation confirm this.

6. Appropriate Use

You agree not to misuse the website, interfere with its operation, or present its results as a professional psychological diagnosis or scientifically validated evaluation.

7. Availability and Changes

This is an educational project and its questions, scoring rules, descriptions, and features may change as the project develops. The website may occasionally be unavailable while updates are made.

8. Limitations

The assessment is provided for educational and self-reflection purposes. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the project owner does not guarantee that the results will be complete, accurate, or suitable for every individual's circumstances.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits any rights or protections that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.
